## Balanced Scorecard (BSC) — Kaplan/Norton

### Four Perspectives

| Perspective | Key Measures |
|-------------|-------------|
| Financial | Revenue growth, cost reduction, asset utilization |
| Customer | Market share, retention, satisfaction, profitability |
| Internal Processes | Innovation → Operations → After-sales service |
| Learning & Growth | Employee skills, info systems, motivation |

### Cause-Effect Chain
```
Learning → Processes → Clients → Finance
```
Every indicator must link to strategy through this chain.

### Leading vs Lagging Indicators

| Type | Meaning | Use |
|------|---------|-----|
| Leading | Predict future | Proactive management |
| Lagging | Reflect past | Historical tracking |

**Both needed** — leading for prediction, lagging for verification.

### Four Barriers to Strategy Execution

1. **Vision not actionable** — strategy not translated to operational terms
2. **Strategy not linked to individual goals** — people don't know their impact
3. **Strategy not linked to resource allocation** — budget doesn't reflect priorities
4. **Feedback is tactical, not strategic** — no indicator-strategy correlation

### BSC Implementation (8 Tasks)

1. Choose organizational unit
2. Establish corporation linkage
3. Conduct first interview round
4. Summary conference
5. Top management seminar (round 1)
6. Subgroup meetings
7. Top management seminar (round 2)
8. Create implementation plan

## PM Maturity Model (PMMM) — Kerzner

| Level | Name | Focus | Key Risk |
|-------|------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Common Language | PM terminology | No leadership support |
| 2 | Common Processes | Repeatable processes | Resistance to change |
| 3 | Unified Methodology | Integrated in culture | Integration complexity |
| 4 | Benchmarking | Compare with others | Measurement difficulty |
| 5 | Continuous Improvement | Learn from experience | Loss of focus |

### Gap Analysis Process

1. Determine current maturity level
2. Determine target level
3. Identify gaps
4. Develop gap-closing plan
5. Implement changes
6. Reassess

### Critical Success Factors

| Category | Factors |
|----------|---------|
| Qualitative | Leadership support, culture, communication |
| Organizational | Structure, resources, training, motivation |
| Quantitative | PM ROI, quality, schedule/budget compliance, customer satisfaction |

## Strategic Management Eras (Шеховцева)

| Era | Environment | Planning Type |
|-----|-------------|---------------|
| Mass production | Stable | Operational |
| Mass sales | Dynamic | Long-term |
| Post-industrial | Threatening | Strategic planning |
| Current | Highly threatening | Strategic management |

### Ansoff Instability Scale

| Score | Management Type | Environment |
|-------|----------------|-------------|
| 0-1.5 | Operational | Familiar, slow changes |
| 1.5-2.5 | Long-term | Extrapolation viable |
| 2.5-3.5 | Strategic planning | Predictable changes |
| 3.5+ | Strategic management | Unpredictable changes |

## Key Balance Points (Фаэй)

| Tension | Balance |
|---------|---------|
| Short vs long term | Both matter |
| Sales growth vs cost control | Context-dependent |
| Market satisfaction vs competitor focus | Both needed |
| Low price vs high quality | Positioning choice |
| Vision vs flexibility | Both required |

**"Both/and" better than "either/or"**

## Planning Principles (Ляско)

1. Unity (holism) — systemic, interconnected
2. Participation — involve members
3. Continuity — plans succeed each other
4. Flexibility — adapt to circumstances
5. Precision — appropriate detail
