## Toyota Way (14 Principles)

### Section 1: Long-Term Philosophy
1. Base decisions on long-term philosophy

### Section 2: Right Process → Right Results
2. Create continuous process flow
3. Use pull systems (avoid overproduction)
4. Level the workload
5. Build a culture of stopping to fix problems
6. Standardize tasks for continuous improvement
7. Use visual controls
8. Use only reliable, tested technology

### Section 3: Develop People & Partners
9. Grow leaders who live the philosophy
10. Develop exceptional people and teams
11. Respect your extended network of partners

### Section 4: Continuous Improvement
12. Go and see for yourself (genchi genbutsu)
13. Make decisions slowly by consensus
14. Become a learning organization through relentless reflection

## 7 Wastes (Muda)

1. Overproduction
2. Waiting
3. Unnecessary transport
4. Overprocessing
5. Inventory
6. Unnecessary movement
7. Defects

## Kanban Formula

```
Kanban quantity = (demand × lead time × safety factor) / container size
```

## Kaizen vs Hoshin

| Kaizen (Evolution) | Hoshin (Revolution) |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| Small steps | Breakthroughs |
| Existing products/processes | New products/markets |
| Attention to detail | Innovative thinking |

## Decision Matrix

| Problem | Approach | Avoid |
|---------|----------|-------|
| Inventory | Kanban pull system | Push scheduling |
| Waste | 7 wastes analysis | Accept waste |
| Workplace | 5S | Disorder |
| Process improvement | Kaizen (incremental) | Waiting for revolution |
| New market | Hoshin (breakthrough) | Incremental only |
