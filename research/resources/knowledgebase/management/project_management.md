## Key Laws

1. **Brooks' Law**: Adding people to late project makes it later
2. **1/3 Rule**: 1/3 planning, 1/6 coding, 1/4 component testing, 1/4 system testing
3. **No Silver Bullet**: No single 10x productivity gain exists
4. **Upstream Investment**: Each QA hour saves 5-20 hours downstream

## Decision Matrix

| Problem | Solution | Anti-pattern |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| Behind schedule | Cut scope, slip schedule | Add people |
| Complex system | Small architect team | Design by committee |
| Defects found late | More upstream reviews | More testing |
| Unknown progress | Miniature milestones | % complete guesses |
| Risk ignored | Top 10 list, weekly review | "Nothing will go wrong" |
