# Knowledge Base: Quality — ISO 9001

**Synthesized from:** Schlickman ISO 9001, Пич ISO 9001 Pocket Guide, ISO 9001

| Concept | Definition | Application |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| QMS | Quality management system | Customer satisfaction |

| PDCA | Plan-Do-Check-Act | Applied to QMS |

## ISO 9001:2000 Structure

| Section | Focus |
|---------|-------|
| 4. QMS | General requirements, documentation |
| 5. Management Responsibility | Commitment, customer focus, planning, review |
| 6. Resource Management | HR, infrastructure, work environment |
| 7. Product Realization | Design, purchasing, production, monitoring |
| 8. Measurement & Improvement | Monitoring, nonconformance, analysis, improvement |

## 8 Quality Management Principles

1. Customer focus
2. Leadership
3. Involvement of people
4. Process approach
5. System approach to management
6. Continual improvement
7. Factual approach to decision making
8. Mutually beneficial supplier relationships

## Three Pillars of QMS

```
1. DOCUMENTATION → Quality Manual → Process Docs → Procedures → Forms/Records
2. IMPLEMENTATION → Operational use of documents daily
3. DEMONSTRATION OF EFFECTIVENESS → Monitor, measure, analyze + corrective/preventive action
```

## PDCA Cycle

```
Plan → establish objectives and processes
Do → implement processes
Check → monitor against policy, objectives, targets
Act → take actions to improve
```

## Mandatory Documents

- Quality policy
- Quality objectives
- Quality manual
- 6 mandatory procedures (4.2.3, 4.2.4, 8.2.2, 8.3, 8.5.2, 8.5.3)
- Work instructions (as needed)
- Records (as needed)

## Key Principle

**Registration ≠ Product quality** — QMS certification confirms system capability, not product quality.

## Decision Matrix

| Problem | ISO 9001 Section | Action |
|---------|-----------------|--------|
| No QMS | 4.1 | Establish general requirements |
| Poor documentation | 4.2 | Quality manual + procedures |
| Leadership gaps | 5.0 | Management commitment |
| Skill gaps | 6.2 | Training and competence |
| Design issues | 7.3 | Design controls |
| Defects | 8.3 | Nonconformance control |
| No improvement | 8.5 | Continual improvement |
