СМЕКАЛКА (Smekalka)
Smekalka (Смекалка): The uniquely human ability to solve complex, seemingly impossible problems through lateral thinking, deep ingenuity, and an absolute refusal to accept compromises.

Smekalka is a fully autonomous, multi-agent engineering and invention architecture. It represents the synthesis of the peak of human problem-solving methodologies—combining rigorous systems engineering, strict requirements analysis, and mathematical invention—executed at machine speed.

It is designed to ingest raw information, define the exact parameters of a system, autonomously invent solutions to technical contradictions, and carry a project through to execution without manual coding or traditional SWE grunt work.

🏗 System Architecture & The Flow of Thought
Smekalka does not just generate text or code; it engineers solutions through a strict, multi-stage pipeline modeled on the most robust frameworks in human history.

1. Ingestion & Deep Research
The system accepts raw inputs, domain constraints, reference materials, and historical data (from modern APIs down to digitized 1970s scientific literature). The assimilation agents study the domain until total context mastery is achieved.

2. The Volere Engine & The Brown Cow Process
Before any solutions are proposed, Smekalka rigorously defines the problem space using Volere requirements engineering methodologies. The swarm moves the problem through the Brown Cow Model:

Current How: Understanding the existing flawed physical implementation.
Current What: Extracting the pure essence of what the current system actually does.
Future What: Defining the Ideal Final Result (IFR)—what the new system must do, stripped of all technical constraints.
Future How: Proposing the new physical/software implementation.
3. The TRIZ Forge
When the leap from Future What to Future How encounters a physical, software, or logical contradiction, the problem is routed to the TRIZ (Theory of Inventive Problem Solving) agents.

Instead of accepting compromises, the swarm utilizes the 39x39 Contradiction Matrix, the 40 Inventive Principles, and Substance-Field Analysis to fundamentally break the paradox.
4. The Quality Gateway & Circular Refinement
Proposed solutions are subjected to a strict Quality Gateway. Solutions are scrutinized against the original Volere requirements.

If a solution fails, it is piped back into the TRIZ Forge.
This creates a high-speed, closed-loop debate among the agents until the exact right architecture is mathematically and logically proven.
5. The Idea Vault (Cold Storage)
During the high-speed lateral thinking process, the TRIZ agents will generate brilliant but currently unnecessary inventions. Instead of discarding them, every unused architectural concept, patentable idea, or alternative solution is permanently logged and archived in the Vault for future extraction.

6. Final Execution
Once the Quality Gateway is cleared, the system proceeds with the actual development process. It moves forward using all, some, or none of the TRIZ interventions based on what the strictest logic dictates, generating the final assets, architectures, or code autonomously.

⚙️ The Swarm Dynamics
Smekalka replaces the traditional SWE and engineering hierarchy. The human acts purely as the Lead Architect/Director, holding the complete vision. The machine executes the heavy lifting, orchestrating the personas required (Systems Thinker, Domain Expert, Requirements Analyst, TRIZ Master, Devil's Advocate) to debate, refine, and produce the final outputs at light speed.
